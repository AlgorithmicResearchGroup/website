The question

What happens when the platform decides your work should disappear?

Make the publication independent of the platform. Keep a signed original and give readers more than one way to find and verify it.

No platform should hold the only copy.

Most online writing lives at an address someone else controls. A host can remove the page, a moderator can suspend the account, an employer can withdraw access, or a service can simply close. When the platform holds the only copy and the only route to readers, its decision can erase the publication with it.

Presskeep starts somewhere else: with a complete, signed HTML document saved on the writer’s computer. The file can be emailed, copied to a drive, published on another website, or opened in an ordinary browser. A platform may refuse to carry it. It does not have to be the work’s sole custodian.

Publish without asking permission to stay published.

Presskeep is a desktop publisher and reader for independent writers, reporters, researchers, and civic organizers. Start with a draft, bring in Markdown, or import selected posts from a Substack export or public feed. Imported work stays private until you decide what to publish.

Publishing saves and verifies the original before contacting any destination. From there, the same exact document can move through websites, resilient archives, direct sharing, or removable media. The app reports each route separately and lets failed transfers be retried without taking successful copies away.

The publication has an identity of its own. Every edition carries proof of where it came from, whether its contents changed, and how it connects to earlier versions. Readers follow that identity rather than a URL, find later editions across changing locations, and keep verified copies for offline reading.

The design challenge was to make independence feel ordinary: a writing surface, a preview, one publish button, and a clear account of where the work went.

01 / Take your work with you

Leave without starting over.

Import Markdown, a Substack export, or a public newsletter feed. Choose the posts you want and review them as private drafts before publishing.

02 / Keep the original

Your publication starts with you.

Presskeep saves the complete signed document locally before any network request. A failed or hostile host cannot take that original with it.

03 / Follow the publication

The writer, not the website.

A follow code connects readers to the publication across changing addresses. Every downloaded edition is verified before it joins their offline library.

04 / More than one route

One removal is not erasure.

Distribute exact copies through websites, archives, direct sharing, and ordinary files. Readers can verify the work independently of whoever delivered it.

Censorship-resistant, not censorship-proof.

No software can guarantee that writing remains reachable everywhere. Networks can be blocked, archives can lose copies, and availability still depends on people retaining and sharing the file. Presskeep does not provide strong anonymity, and a signature does not prove that an author’s claims are true.

What it removes is a single point of control. One company can close an account. One host can remove a page. Neither has to possess the only copy, the only history, or the only path back to the reader.

Presskeep is available for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 11 or later, and for x86-64 Linux. It requires no account, activation server, or subscription. The project website also includes a signed sample document anyone can download and open.

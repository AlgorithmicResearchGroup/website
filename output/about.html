Algorithmic Research Group / At ARG Studio

The research practice.

A small lab doing work that needs to exist.

Algorithmic Research Group builds tools and infrastructure for AI safety research. Benchmarks for evaluating autonomous agents. Datasets for studying how models fail. Runtimes for running agent workloads at scale.

Open output

We build benchmarks, datasets, and infrastructure for evaluating what autonomous AI systems can actually do. The research output is open source so other researchers, auditors, and teams can inspect and use it.

For-profit, public-interest

Algorithmic Research Group is a for-profit research lab. Consulting work and sponsorships fund its open-source research. The structure keeps the work lean and self-directed; publishing the output makes it available for independent evaluation.

Support the research

Sponsorships fund open-source benchmarks, datasets, and tooling for AI security research. Everything built with sponsor support is released publicly.

Work together

For funded research projects, dataset sponsorships, or infrastructure partnerships, start a conversation. We're flexible on structure and committed to keeping the research output open.

We work with founders, teams, and researchers who need a thoughtful partner to turn an idea into something real. The shape of the project can vary. The work stays hands-on.

## Product design & development

Apps, websites, and tools with a clear point of view. We can help shape the idea, design the experience, and build the software, keeping the details connected from first prototype to release.

## AI systems

Useful applications of AI, built around the people who use them. That might mean a voice interface, an agent, a document workflow, or a custom tool that fits into an existing product. We start with what the system needs to do and test it against real examples.

## Research & prototyping

Experiments that help you decide what comes next. We build prototypes, evaluation tools, and research infrastructure to explore technical questions and make uncertain ideas easier to assess.

## A close working relationship

We start by understanding the idea, the constraints, and what a useful first version would look like. Then we make something tangible, share it early, and refine it together.

You work directly with the people doing the work. Scope is deliberate, progress is visible, and technical decisions come with a reason.

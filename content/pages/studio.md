ARG Studio is where I make independent software, explore new ideas, and work with people on things worth building.

The work moves between design, software development, and AI research. Sometimes that means a small, opinionated app. Sometimes it means a custom system for a client. Sometimes it starts with a question and a prototype to see where it goes.

## A few things in orbit

[Saylight]({filename}/pages/apps/saylight.md) keeps voice transcription on the Mac instead of sending recordings to a cloud service. [Harness]({filename}/pages/apps/harness.md) puts local, visible controls around AI-agent work. [Presskeep]({filename}/pages/apps/presskeep.md) gives writers signed, portable publications that do not depend on one platform’s permission.

[ELSEWHERE]({filename}/pages/apps/elsewhere.md) searches apps, documents, notes, and clipboard history locally without uploading queries, filenames, or an index. [Dropbeam]({filename}/pages/apps/dropbeam.md) sends files over an authenticated encrypted connection using a one-time code instead of an account or cloud folder.

[Algorithmic Research Group]({filename}/pages/research/index.md) develops benchmarks, datasets, and open infrastructure for AI safety research.

Different subjects, shared instincts: keep data and control close to the person using the software, make the system understandable, and avoid turning dependence into the business model.

## The person behind it

I teach AI at Duke, with courses in applied AI, AI products, and software development. Previously, I was a Senior ML Engineer at Apple and worked at several startups, building software that had to hold up in everyday use.

My research focuses on AI systems and agents. I've raised over $1.5 million in research grants and presented at NeurIPS and ICML. Research informs how I build; building gives me better questions to research.

## Small on purpose

I stay close to the work, from the first sketch to the code that ships. When a project needs more depth, I bring in collaborators with the right expertise.

For clients, that means a direct working relationship, visible progress, and honest conversations about what to build next. For the studio, it means enough space to pursue our own ideas, too.
